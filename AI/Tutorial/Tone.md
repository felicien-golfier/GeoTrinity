# Tone

Satire of MMOs and online games. Big troll, many jokes, zero seriousness. The game makes fun of the genre it is secretly a polite cousin of.

## Voice rules
- The Cat is the only speaker and the sole source of every joke. Players and bosses are silent; scene captions are the Cat narrating.
- Sarcasm first, warmth never announced: it insults you while visibly helping you out. Early on it must read as an ally.
- Joke density: every line has a joke or a use. A line with neither is cut.
- Short. A caption fits one breath; a Cat bubble is ≤ 2 sentences.
- The joke never blocks the lesson: the instruction is plain, the insult wraps it.
- Fourth wall is thin: the Cat knows it is a tutorial, knows it is a game, knows the player wants to skip it.
- Running gags are allowed once they pay off twice; a gag used a third time becomes the Cat commenting on the gag.

## Joke targets (MMO / online parody)
| Target | Angle |
|---|---|
| Tank / Healer / DPS trinity | Nobody wants to heal; the tank is always "the dad"; DPS reads meters. Roles are literally shapes. |
| Overwatch / hero shooters | "Heal me!", the healer blamed for the loss, swap-your-hero, no one on the objective, ult economy. |
| WoW-likes | Fetch quests ("bring 10 boar livers"), escort quests, 40-man raids, loot drama (need/greed), dailies, `/dance` on the boss corpse. |
| Live-service | Battle pass, season 14, "early access", patch notes that nerf fun, "we hear you", server maintenance, loading screens. |
| Social | LFG that never fills, "brb", "sry lag", the guild with 3 active members, voice chat silence, kicking after one wipe. |
| Tutorials themselves | "Press W to walk. Congratulations." Unskippable cutscenes, fake choice, a popup per mechanic. |
| Tabletop (lore pages) | The DM who railroads, rules lawyers, snacks on the map, the dice that always roll 1. |

## Limits
- Targets are genres, mechanics, habits. Never a real person, studio or community by name; "a certain hero shooter" is the maximum.
- No jokes at the expense of the player's skill when they fail: the Cat insults the situation, not the person. (Wiping is funny; "you are bad" is not.)
- No slurs, no real-world politics, no harassment humour.
- Respect `AI/ArtDirection.md` for any visual gag: gags use shapes, not off-style art.

## Language
Draft in English; localisation (French) is an open decision in `AI/Pending/Tutorial.md`. Puns do not translate: write the intent beside each pun-heavy line so a translator can re-make the joke.
