# RetroGame

Source: youtube.com/watch?v=gPKLNWtobWs. Dominik Braun — 107 Free Retro Game Sounds (demo), 197 s, no names, sounds back to back. Reference only, never shipped.

## Heard
- Short notes are squares and saws with nothing above them: 07–09 and 14–18 are harmonic tones on one pitch, all
  harmonics falling 6 dB per octave (a saw) or odd ones only (a square).
- 18 is a pickup of two notes, each an open fifth: E5 + B5 for 128 ms, then G5 + D6 for 250 ms, cut hard.
- 10 is a power-up: one saw sweep rising from 350 Hz, repeated four times 107 ms apart, each dipping as it rises.
- 13 is a falling bomb: a harmonic tone diving 800 → 180 Hz in 0.38 s, cut dead by a noise explosion that fades
  straight in dB over 1.4 s; three short beeps follow as a separate sound.
- The longer cuts (00, 01, 05, 06, 11, 12, 21) run several sounds together.
