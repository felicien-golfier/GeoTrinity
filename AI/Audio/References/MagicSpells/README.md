# MagicSpells

Source: youtube.com/watch?v=E0wuIpCF5BE. Daydream Sound — Magic Spells SFX Showcase, 85 s, no names; ice, arcane, fire and shadow casts, whooshes, heal loops, buffs. Reference only, never shipped.

## Heard
- The casts are noise, 0–17 % tone: a swell of 0.9–1.1 s peaking at 0.25–0.45 s, the low end (63–125 Hz) the
  loudest band and flickering 13–28 times a second like a fire, the highs 10–18 dB under it.
- Some carry a sparkle: steady inharmonic lines between 4.9 and 17.5 kHz, 12–25 dB under the peak, a few tenths of
  a second each (33, 34), over the noise; 13 opens bright (8–16 kHz peaking at 48 ms) and darkens.
- They are wide: left and right correlate at only 0.44–0.65, where every sword swish and retro sound is mono.
