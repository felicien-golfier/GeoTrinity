# Swooshes

Source: youtube.com/watch?v=sxpX1FpV6xM. Kaushal Creation — Swoosh Sound Effects Transition Pack, 64 s: a 19 s
intro (cut 00), then "SWOOSH - NN" titles, each swoosh under its own title, and an outro (cut 19). Video-editing
transitions, not game swishes. Reference only, never shipped.

## Heard
- Low and long against a blade: centroids 120–550 Hz, 0.3–3 s, most of the energy under 250 Hz with a slow tail
  (02: 1.35 s, down 20 dB 690 ms after the peak). A game dash or dodge wants the sword swishes' shape; these are
  for a camera move.
