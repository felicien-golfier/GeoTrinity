# UIFuturistic

Source: youtube.com/watch?v=yIDp1YdTiHg. Gladius Audio — Futuristic UI Sound Effects Pack (113 SFX), 34 s; the category is the button the cursor presses (Click, Press, Hover, Confirm, Cancel, Error, Inventory, Notify, Countdown), the file name is too small to read, but the cursor tells the button: 00–02 Click, 03–05 Press, 06–10 Hover, 11–12 Confirm, 13–14 Cancel, 15–17 Error, 18–21 Inventory, 22–23 Notify, 24 Countdown. Reference only, never shipped.

## Heard
- Confirm (12) is a rising arpeggio of short pure tones, about 30 ms each from 1.1 kHz up past 4 kHz in 120 ms,
  each note 2–4 dB under the one before, then an echo every 60 ms ringing the first pitch on for 0.3 s.
- Error (15) is a low buzz: saw-coloured notes on ~260 Hz, stuttered into three or four pulses over 0.4 s under a
  23 Hz flutter of 11 dB, with thumps under 100 Hz on each pulse. Low, rough and repeated reads as refused.
- Click (01, 02) is one tone gliding: 520 → 1230 Hz over 180 ms, or 540 → 380 Hz over 140 ms, nearly pure.
- Hover (06, 09) is an 18 ms tick of noise at the top of the spectrum; Countdown (24) is a near-pure 709 Hz sine
  held 310 ms; Notify (23) holds 1040 Hz for half a second over its lower harmonics.
