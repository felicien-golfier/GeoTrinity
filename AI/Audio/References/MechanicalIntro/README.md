# Mechanical intro reference — cut apart

Source: youtube.com/watch?v=NHu-wEb6SGI, "Mechanical Sound FX — 110 Industrial Sound Samples" (djdrops.eu), 71 s.
One continuous produced track, no silences: a sub drone bed with mechanical layers on top.
`Mech_NN_<Name>.wav` are its sections, cut by eye from the spectrogram with 5 ms fades. Reference only, never shipped.

## The bed under everything
- A sub drone at 28–41 Hz, the loudest band throughout; a bass line moves over it at 90–165 Hz.
- Every mechanical layer sits 5–20 dB under the 30–200 Hz band.
- Before a big entry the bed dips or drops out for 0.1–0.5 s (4.5 s, 43–44 s, 49.5–50 s, 63.3 s); the entry
  then starts hard.

## The grammar
- Metal is snaps and short rings: a broadband click dead within 17–40 ms with one or two ring lines, never a
  pitched body or a bounce.
- Hits come in flurries of 3–6 within ~300 ms, not one at a time.
- A running machine is a click train of 9–20 a second under steady high lines.
- Every section enters hard after a dip in the bed.
